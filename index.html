Questions

Before you book a demo

What does ₹45–50 per student per year include?

Everything: attendance, fee ledger, exams, teacher payroll, parent WhatsApp alerts and the student app. No setup fees, server charges or extra cost for new batches.

Can teachers work only from their phones?

Yes. Yaqeel is built mobile-first. A teacher can mark a class of 60 or enter test scores from a smartphone, with no laptop needed.

How do parents get updates?

Attendance, marks and fee receipts show in the student app and are also sent to parents as automatic WhatsApp messages.

Is our student and fee data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only authorised administrators and verified faculty can open your institute’s portal.
